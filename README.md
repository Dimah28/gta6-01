Solmar Bay — an original open-world crime game in the browser: drive and steal cars through a living city, dodge a 1–5 star wanted system with police chases and roadblocks, run six story missions and taxi/courier side jobs, and spend your cash at the garage. No Rockstar names, maps or assets — the city, story, art and sound are all original. Runs offline from static files, no libraries, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, E to enter a car or interact, F to punch, click to shoot, SPACE for handbrake, P to pause, M to mute.
