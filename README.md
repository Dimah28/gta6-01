Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. Cruise a living coastal city, steal cars, outrun the law, and build your reputation from small-time hustler to the most wanted name on the bay. Everything — the streets, the story, the cars, and the chaos — is yours to explore, no download or install required.

## Features

- **1–5 star wanted system** — the more heat you bring, the harder the city pushes back.
- **Police chases and roadblocks** — patrol cars hunt you down, spike strips and blockades cut off your escape routes.
- **Six story missions** — a full arc of jobs, betrayals, and big scores across Solmar Bay.
- **Taxi and courier side jobs** — earn honest (and not-so-honest) cash between missions.
- **Garage** — spend your earnings on better rides and upgrades for your getaway cars.
- **Living city** — traffic flows, pedestrians walk the sidewalks, and the streets feel alive.
- **Coastal day atmosphere** — bright seaside light, open roads, and a skyline built for joyriding.
- **Drive and steal cars** — jump into anything with four wheels and make it yours.
- **On-foot action** — punch, shoot, and fight your way out when the car can't save you.

## Run it

No build step, no dependencies to install, no network needed. Just serve the static files and open the game in your browser:

```bash
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

### Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD | Move on foot / drive |
| E | Enter a car or interact |
| F | Punch |
| Click | Shoot |
| SPACE | Handbrake |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |

## About

Solmar Bay is a fully original game. It uses no Rockstar names, maps, or assets — the city, story, art, and sound are all original work created for this project.

The game runs offline from static files with no libraries and no network connection required. 3D rendering is powered by Three.js, and the whole experience loads straight from your local machine into the browser.

Whether you are chasing the next story mission, grinding taxi fares for a better car, or just seeing how long you can survive at five stars, Solmar Bay is built to be picked up and played in seconds — and hard to put down.
